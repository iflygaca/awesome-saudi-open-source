# GACAR and Saudi AIP cross-reference — awesome-saudi-open-source

Checked: **11 November 2026**. This is a maintainer's topic index connecting repository files to GACAR clauses and Saudi AIP sections. A topic match does not approve a claim or establish regulatory compliance. Applications do not automatically consume this document.

Official publishers: [GACA](https://gaca.gov.sa/) · [Saudi eAIP](https://aimss.sans.com.sa/en/eaip).

The [official publication history](https://aimss.sans.com.sa/assets/FileManagerFiles/ebab6118-87c3-4ac0-8c01-1b80f60459f7.html) checked on that date lists **AMDT 11/26, effective 29 October 2026**, as effective and **AMDT 12/26, effective 26 November 2026**, as upcoming. AIP links below intentionally identify AMDT 11/26. Recheck the portal, supplements and NOTAM before using operational information.

## Applicability

General Saudi open-source directory. Listing an aviation project does not make GACAR or AIP provisions requirements of the directory itself.

No direct GACAR or AIP requirement is assigned to this repository. Reassess this scope if aviation lessons, rule-based calculations, charts or operational datasets are introduced. Ownership by @iflygaca alone does not make an aviation rule applicable.

## Maintaining the references

1. Recheck the official listing/history rather than assuming a reachable old PDF or page is the current edition.
2. Record the exact GACAR section and PDF version, or AIP section, ICAO identifier, amendment and effective date for each claim.
3. Retain existing source hashes, hosted provenance, review status and rights restrictions until the content is actually revalidated.
4. Do not use FAA section numbering as a substitute for GACAR section numbering.
5. When a source changes, review affected English and Arabic content and generated/native copies separately.
